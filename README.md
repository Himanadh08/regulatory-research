## 🇮🇳 India Regulations

### DPDP Act 2023
**Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023**  
India's primary data protection legislation. Covers obligations of 
Data Fiduciaries, rights of Data Principals, consent requirements, 
penalties up to ₹250 Cr, and the Data Protection Board of India.  
Compared to GDPR where relevant.

### RBI IT Framework
**Reserve Bank of India — Cyber Security Framework for Banks**  
RBI's IT governance and cybersecurity mandates for scheduled 
commercial banks. Covers IT governance structure, information 
security requirements, incident reporting, and IT audit obligations.

### SEBI Cyber Security Framework
**Securities and Exchange Board of India — Cyber Resilience Framework**  
Cybersecurity requirements for stock exchanges, depositories, 
and market intermediaries. Covers governance, risk management, 
incident response, and third-party risk for capital market entities.

## 🌍 Global Frameworks

### NIST CSF 2.0
**NIST Cybersecurity Framework Version 2.0**  
All 6 functions covered: Govern (new in 2.0), Identify, Protect, 
Detect, Respond, Recover. Includes mapping to ISO 27001 clauses 
and key differences from CSF 1.1.

### NIST AI RMF
**NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework**  
Covers the 4 core functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.  
Applied to AI system lifecycle risk assessment.  
Directly relevant to EU AI Act compliance implementation.

### ISO 27001 vs SOC 2 vs NIST CSF
Side-by-side comparison of the three most referenced frameworks 
in GRC interviews. Covers: who uses each, what they test, 
when to recommend which, certification vs attestation vs framework.

### DORA
**Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU) — effective January 2025**  
EU financial sector cyber resilience regulation. Covers 5 pillars: 
ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, 
third-party ICT risk, and information sharing. Mandatory for all 
EU banks, insurance firms, and investment companies.

### EU AI Act
**European Union Artificial Intelligence Act**  
Risk classification system: Unacceptable / High / Limited / Minimal. 
Key obligations for high-risk AI systems. Transparency requirements. 
Enforcement timelines including August 2026 applicability date.

### GDPR vs DPDP vs CCPA
Comparison of three major data protection regimes:  
EU GDPR · India DPDP Act 2023 · California CCPA.  
Covers: territorial scope, consent requirements, data subject rights, 
breach notification timelines, and penalty structures.

### ISA/IEC 62443
**OT and ICS Security Standard**  
Conceptual overview of the primary Operational Technology security 
standard. Covers Purdue Reference Model, IT vs OT security differences, 
and relevance to power sector and maritime GRC.

## 🏭 Sector-Specific Regulations

### ICAO Aviation Cybersecurity
**ICAO Doc 10099 — Aviation Cybersecurity Strategy**  
6-pillar framework for civil aviation cybersecurity governance 
globally. Studied alongside India's DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements 
on Cybersecurity.

### Healthcare GRC Overview
Covers: FDA 21 CFR Part 11 (electronic records in pharma), 
GxP principles (GMP, GLP, GCP), HIPAA overview, and DPDP Act 
application to patient data in Indian healthcare.

### IMO Maritime Cyber Guidelines
**IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 — Maritime Cyber Risk Management**  
High-level recommendations for maritime cyber risk management 
in Safety Management Systems. Studied alongside BIMCO guidelines.

## 📊 Comparisons

### DORA vs RBI IT Framework
Direct comparison of EU financial cyber regulation (DORA) with 
India's RBI IT Framework. Covers structural differences, 
overlapping requirements, and gaps relevant to multinational 
banks operating across both jurisdictions.

### ISO 27001 vs NIST CSF Mapping
Control-level mapping between ISO 27001 Annex A and NIST CSF 
functions. Useful for organisations implementing one framework 
and needing to demonstrate alignment with the other.
