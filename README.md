## 🇮🇳 India Regulations

### DPDP Act 2023
India's primary data protection legislation. Covers obligations of 
Data Fiduciaries, rights of Data Principals, consent requirements, 
penalties up to ₹250 Cr, and the Data Protection Board of India.

### IT Act 2000
India's foundational cybersecurity and electronic commerce law. 
Covers offences, penalties, intermediary liability, and CERT-In's 
role in national cyber incident response.

### CERT-In Directions 2022
CERT-In's mandatory cybersecurity directions for Indian organisations. 
Covers incident reporting timelines (6 hours), log retention (180 days), 
VPN provider obligations, and KYC requirements for cloud and VPN services.

### RBI Cybersecurity Framework
RBI's IT governance and cybersecurity mandates for scheduled commercial 
banks. Covers IT governance structure, information security requirements, 
incident reporting, and IT audit obligations.

### RBI Digital Payment Security Guidelines
RBI's security requirements for digital payment systems. Covers 
authentication standards, fraud monitoring, card tokenisation, 
and customer data protection for payment service providers.

### SEBI Cybersecurity Framework
Cybersecurity requirements for stock exchanges, depositories, and market 
intermediaries. Covers governance, risk management, incident response, 
and third-party risk for capital market entities.

### IRDAI Cybersecurity Guidelines
Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India cybersecurity 
requirements for insurance companies. Covers data governance, business 
continuity, and cyber risk management for the insurance sector.

### NCIIPC — Critical Information Infrastructure Protection
National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre framework 
for protecting India's critical sectors — power, banking, telecom, 
transport, government, and strategic enterprises.

### India Cyber Regulatory Landscape
Consolidated overview of how all Indian cyber regulations interconnect — 
DPDP Act, IT Act, CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and NCIIPC — and how 
GRC professionals navigate multi-regulator compliance in India.

## 🌍 Global Frameworks

### NIST CSF 2.0
All 6 functions: Govern (new in 2.0), Identify, Protect, Detect, 
Respond, Recover. Includes mapping to ISO 27001 and differences from CSF 1.1.

### NIST AI RMF
4 core functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Applied to AI system 
lifecycle risk assessment. Directly relevant to EU AI Act compliance.

### DORA
EU financial sector cyber resilience regulation effective January 2025. 
5 pillars: ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, 
third-party ICT risk, and information sharing.

### EU AI Act
Risk classification: Unacceptable / High / Limited / Minimal. 
Key obligations for high-risk AI systems. High-risk requirements 
applicable from August 2026.

### GDPR vs DPDP vs CCPA
Comparison of EU GDPR, India DPDP Act 2023, and California CCPA. 
Covers territorial scope, consent, data subject rights, breach 
notification, and penalty structures.

### IEC 62443
Primary OT and ICS security standard. Covers Purdue Reference Model, 
IT vs OT security differences, and relevance to power sector and 
maritime GRC.
